Quick guide

Playing with SAN

Type the destination square for a pawn, or the piece letter followed by its square.

e4
Move a pawn to e4
Nf3
Move a knight to f3
Bxc6
A bishop captures on c6
O-O
Castle kingside
Qh7#
Queen moves to h7 with checkmate

The board is display-only in Phase 0. Moves are entered in the field.
